*Rough notes for now. I will come back and fill this in.*

I was reading Matthew 25, the parable of the talents. A master goes away and leaves each of his servants with something to look after. Not the same amount for each, but something for every one of them. When he comes back, the question he asks is not how much they were given. It is what they did with it.

That stayed with me. I started asking what has been put in my hands.

For me, right now, it is two things: building websites and writing.

Neither feels like much. But the servant who was commended was not the one with the most. He was the one who was faithful with what he had.

So this site is my small attempt at that. A place to write down what God puts on my heart, and a place to build.

More to come.
